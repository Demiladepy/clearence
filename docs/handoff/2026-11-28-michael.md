**4. What a reviewer will catch** (found by building your branch and clicking through it):
- **The landing "Try it" still runs a mock:** `src/services/clearance.ts` says `// Mock` and
  `TryIt` uses it with a hardcoded ₦50,000. We say "no mocks" everywhere, and a reviewer who opens
  the repo will see this first. Either remove it or make it read the live NG pack.
- **The sign-up form collects email and password and ignores them.** A form that does nothing
  reads as fake. Make the first screen honest: "Connect a Stellar wallet" or "Use demo account".
- **The KYC request only works when the user and operator share a browser** (it travels through
  `localStorage`). Show the user's Clearance ID with a copy button on Verify, and add a "paste ID"
  field in the console. Then it works across devices, and the recording shows two real parties.
- **The operator should sign with Freighter, not a pasted secret.** "The operator's keys never
  touch our app" is part of the product story. Keep the record key in memory only.

**5. Design.** Straight feedback: the landing page still looks generated. It isn't any one choice;
it's the combination, which is the default look these tools produce:
- cream paper with notebook lines;
- Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Sans and IBM Plex Mono;
- a mono uppercase eyebrow over every section;
- hard 8px offset shadows and rubber "CLEARED / NOT SENT" stamps;
- 01 / 02 / 03 steps;
- a three-line headline ending in a full stop.

People who have seen a lot of these spot it in a second, and the reviewers have.

Concrete bugs on the page today (1440px):
- the payment-record card is cut off by the phone card ("TIER" and "Passed" are clipped);
- "NOT SENT" floats on its own;
- the three How-it-works columns step down at different heights;
- the code sample is clipped on the right;
- on a phone the nav wraps to three lines and the hero art disappears.

Direction, in the spirit of Stripe, Mercury, Ramp or Linear: infrastructure that looks calm and
shows the real thing.
- **One sans family with tabular figures** (Geist + Geist Mono is free and fine), plain white or
  near-white background, one accent colour. No paper texture, no stamps, no offset shadows, no mono
  eyebrows.
- **Real product, not illustrations.** The hero shows actual screenshots of the wallet and the
  operator console with an opened record, framed plainly.
- **Proof instead of claims.** This is our edge over teams who bolted Stellar onto an existing
  product: everything we have is live, and we can show it.
  - A thin strip under the hero, read live from testnet: "Last payment verified on Stellar N
    minutes ago · active rule pack NG-v1 · pool contract", each linking to stellar.expert.
  - The Problem section uses two real explorer views: a public Stellar payment (who, whom, how much)
    next to a real Clearance pool transaction (a proof and nothing else). Real data, not grey bars.
- **Copy for a product, not a hackathon.** Drop "Stellar Buildathon 2026" from the eyebrow and
  footer. Lead with who it's for and the one thing it does: licensed apps in Nigeria and Ghana
  sending private payments their regulator can still audit.
- **Fewer sections, each earning its place:** problem, how it works (with the real console
  screenshot), rule packs (live from chain), developers (working code), then a call to action.
